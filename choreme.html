Privacy Policy

ChoreMe — Last updated October 2026

ChoreMe is a chore tracking app built for personal use. This policy explains how your data is handled.

Data collection

ChoreMe does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. All information you enter (chore names, schedules, reminders) is stored locally on your device and never leaves it.

Third-party services

ChoreMe does not use any analytics, advertising, or third-party SDKs that collect data.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, the app schedules local notifications on your device. No data is sent to any server.

Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, you can reach out via the Google Play listing.
